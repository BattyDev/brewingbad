A PLACE TO MAKE YOUR OWN

Small workshop.
Room for friends.

Turn a bare corner into a place to grow, grind, brew and bottle. Put what you earn into growing. No rent, wages or upkeep.

  • 01

    One host, three friends

    Up to four players share the rank, bank and workshop. Your purse is your own; leave coin and items on a shelf to pass them along.

  • 02

    A day and a night together

    Days last about 20 minutes. At 4 AM the clock waits until everyone sleeps. Group XP is tallied overnight; morning starts at 7.

  • 03

    Beyond the gate

    Earn gate papers together and take your brewing into the Lower Market. Everyone needs the same build to join.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

What kind of game is Brewing Bad?＋

A first-person alchemy and workshop game. Arrive by ferry, rent an empty byre, grow and prepare ingredients by hand, and brew remedies. Earn rank and coin for gate papers into the Lower Market.

When can I play?＋

A Windows PC demo is planned. There isn't a public release date yet. The download will appear in the demo section when it's ready.

Can I play with friends?＋

The current development build supports one host and up to three friends using the host’s address over LAN or a configured internet connection. You share rank, the bank and the workshop, while keeping your own purse. Everyone needs the exact same build. Public demo multiplayer details remain in development.

Is this what the game really looks like?＋

Yes. These are actual development-build captures with the HUD hidden. The four-player video uses one host and three connected joiners performing the game’s emotes. Equipment, stock, gate access and emotes are staged for filming. Morra and Hazel in the header are the actual game models, posed and rendered in Blender. Visuals and features may change during development.
