A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

What kind of game is Brewing Bad?＋

A first-person alchemy and workshop game. Grow and gather ingredients, prepare them by hand, brew remedies, and fill customer requests to develop your apothecary.

When can I play?＋

A Windows PC demo is planned. There isn't a public release date yet. The download will appear in the demo section when it's ready.

Can I play with friends?＋

The prototype supports one host and up to three guests on a trusted local network. Online matchmaking and Steam invitations are not available in the current build. Demo multiplayer details will be confirmed before release.

Is this what the game really looks like?＋

Yes. The screenshots come from the playable development build. Morra and Hazel in the header are the actual game models, posed and rendered in Blender. Visuals and features may change during development.
